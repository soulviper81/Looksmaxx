ABOUT LOOKSMAXX

Private by default.

Face landmarks and quality metrics are processed in the browser for this build. No camera frames are uploaded to a server.

The app uses a landmark-based heuristic. It is designed to provide structured appearance feedback, not medical, psychological or scientific judgments.
