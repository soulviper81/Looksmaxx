Simple improvements.

Use repeatable conditions when you compare photos over time.

☼

Front-facing light

Use soft daylight facing you rather than harsh overhead or back lighting.

⌖

Stable framing

Keep the camera close to eye level and avoid extreme lens or angle changes.

✦

Consistent baseline

Track grooming and style changes with similar distance, light and expression.
